About Us

Texas Hamburger Palace is a restaurant located in Houston, Texas. We offer a variety of food options including burgers, sandwiches, salads, side orders, and drinks

Our History

Texas is big but its history with hamburgers is bigger! Texas Hamburger Day was proclaimed by Governor Bill Clements.

The proclamation recognized claims that the first hamburger was cooked in Athens, Texas, by Fletcher "Old Dave" Davis. It was also introduced at the 1904 St. Louis World's Fair.
